# Progress

What has landed, newest first. One line per change; the PR has the detail.

## 2026-10-13

- Limits. Each person gets 10 AI uses a day, shared by everything that calls a model: a note drafted for a moment, a typed or spoken request, a journal title. The day starts at midnight UTC. After that, moments still save without a drafted note, and the box and voice say so. Link previews have their own hourly limit, since they aren't AI. Every change a person makes passes one limit (300 a minute, refilling), which stops a client stuck in a loop. Settings → Account shows AI uses left today, storage against the 2 GB, and experiences against the 20.
- Search and link previews. Every page now has its own title, description and 1200×630 preview card for X, Reddit, Discord, Slack, iMessage and WhatsApp: the site, a shared trip, and an invite. Cards are drawn by `scripts/og-images.mjs`, in the style of a dark card with hairline frame. A shared trip's card never names the trip. Only the home page is indexed: the rest answer `noindex` in a tag and an `X-Robots-Tag` header. There's a sitemap, and `robots.txt` adds content signals (search and AI answers yes, training no).
- For AI agents: `/llms.txt` says what Story-time is, what's private and what agents should leave alone, and the home page answers `Accept: text/markdown` with the same text.
- The home page's first screen comes in the HTML, so it shows before the script loads (server rendering is still off); someone already signed in doesn't see it on the way to /home. Grey text on the landing page is lighter, for contrast. Lighthouse on mobile: accessibility 96 → 100, SEO and best practices 100.

## 2026-10-12

- The app reads Convex through TanStack Query (`@convex-dev/react-query`). `src/lib/queries.ts` has one factory per feature (`queries.moments.list({ eventId })`), and the experience, journal, story and share routes prefetch theirs in a `loader`, so hovering a link starts its data and the page and loader share one live cache entry. Prefetching waits until sign-in is known. Errors still reach the error screen. Lint rejects `useQuery` from `convex/react`. Writes still use Convex's `useMutation`, and the two paginated lists keep `usePaginatedQuery`.
- Functions are arrow functions everywhere: `const name = () => {}`, never `function name() {}`. 661 declarations were converted, and lint (`func-style`) now fails on a new one. The 17 route files define `Route` last, after the components it names.
- **convex/ reorganized** (after ianmacartney/code-organization-pattern). Functions are written in `convex/model/` and leave a file only inside its `…Api` object; `convex/api/` holds one-line re-exports, which is the API the app calls (`api.moments.list`, from `@/lib/api`). Helpers stay in `convex/lib/`. `convex/README.md` maps every file for people and agents, and `convex/tests/layout.test.ts` fails if the layout or the map drifts. Internal functions are `internal.model.*`; the seed is `model/seed:japan`.
- Lists and thumbnails fetch a stored 480px JPEG, and only once the picture is near the screen. The open page still fetches the original for the photos you can see. A photo from before this falls back to the original.
- Printing a journal writes one page at a time into the file, on one canvas, at a lower scale and JPEG quality. The button shows how far it has got, and Cancel stops the rest.
- The first paint no longer downloads the scrapbook fonts or MapLibre. Manrope and Newsreader are preloaded. The map, the film, and the move handles load when they are actually on screen.
- A confirmed email is the reply address and the place a memory reminder goes (PR #49). Sign-in copies a verified address. One typed in Settings waits until its link is opened. Share links can be emailed. Once a day, if a past year has this date and the owner asked, the mail says a memory is waiting and links home, with nothing about the memory.
- A share link can leave photos out. Pictures, stickers, a link card's preview image — including an http one — and the locked PDF's id stay off the public payload. The owner still sees them, and switching it off brings them back.
- **Share the journal as a locked PDF.** Export → "Share as a locked PDF…" prints the whole book in the browser, locks it with the PDF standard's AES-256 encryption and a password (generated, or your own), and puts it on the share link, where a PDF button downloads it. Any reader asks for the password; the link never gives it out. The file is stored encrypted like photos; the password is kept under the experience's key so the owner can copy it again, replace the PDF or take it off. Also fixed: exporting a PDF stopped on its first page (the offstage sheet was looked up before it existed).
- **Journal gaps closed.** A title you retyped is yours: "Update this page" keeps it. A trip with no dates keeps its cover's dates in step as moments are added, moved or deleted. A moment page's title can "Try another" (Haiku, from the moment; nothing stored until you use it). Cover dates now print the calendar day people picked, including UTC+13 and just east of UTC+12, or the day a moment happened where it happened.
- **Experiences in the sidebar.** Every experience is listed under a folding Experiences tab.
- **A seeded trip to Japan** for trying the app: on a dev deployment, `pnpm exec convex run seed:japan '{"userId":"..."}'` makes "Spring in Japan", ten days from Tokyo to Osaka with places, routes, notes, moods and labels, and its journal cuts itself. It refuses production and will not pick an account on its own. No photos.
- Activity shows how many new moments, pages, and guestbook notes arrived since it was last opened. Deleting the trip removes those notes and the read mark. A burst of other changes cannot hide a new one.
- A link preview will not open a hostname that resolves to a private address. The connection is pinned to the addresses just checked, so a later DNS answer cannot move it. Trailing dots, short numeric hosts, and local names are refused before any fetch.
- Pressing / focuses the box only when moment search is not on the page. Search keeps /.
- The box can attach photos already in the trip. They are the ones you uploaded that are not on a moment yet, and they become one moment, with your words as the note.
- Moments that never got a page get one when the journal is opened, and once a day if nobody opens it. A page taken out on purpose stays out.
- A thing locked in place on a journal page stays put. A save cannot move it, rewrite it, or take it off; unlocking it is its own save, and catching a page up leaves it where it was.
- The day it is, for the person reading, is marked Today in the moments list and in the journal's contents.
- Play opens the journal (PR #46). The film is still there, behind Play. A moment with no page falls back to the story, and a page that clipped the note offers "Read the moment". Taking a sticker back was already in PR #39; the smaller on-device model is still to try.
- The cover keeps the trip's name and dates until someone retypes that line (PR #45). A rename after that leaves the handmade cover alone.
- Past a few dozen pages, printing the whole journal is named as the long choice (PR #44); this page and this day stay the usual ones.
- A moment's page can catch up (PR #43). Until the owner saves it, opening the book updates the prints and the note. After that, the page says "The moment changed." and waits for "Update this page", which keeps what they added. A deleted moment stays on the page and says so. A tier list that doesn't fit continues on the next page. A missing photo reads "This photo was removed".
- The journal's page strip is a contents, grouped by day (PR #42). The open page and its neighbours are drawn small; the rest stay a label.
- A share link opens the journal as a book (PR #41). Play still starts the film. A sticker's bytes come through the link only when a page shows that sticker.
- A moment's page is cut on the server when the moment is saved, cover included (PR #40). A journal that already has 400 pages says so on that moment's row instead of promising a page.
- Journal review fixes (PR #39): a full book no longer skips moments that never got a page, a locked page stays put, a share link only fetches a sticker that is on a page, and a sticker can leave the shelf once every page lets it go.
- Deleting an experience also removes a connection token that was limited to it (PR #37). Account-wide tokens stay until A2. The 1,500 media ceiling is unchanged.
- **#34 and #35 merged** to master (not deployed yet). The local `story-time` MCP connector is removed. The next features are planned in `tasks.md`.
- **Moments page shows the journal (PR #35).** On a wide screen the space beside the list shows the picked moment's journal page, or the cover, with Play from here, Edit and Open in the journal (which opens on that page). On a phone a row still opens the editor or the story. The header lines up with the content.
- **Journal: zoom, draw, add anything (PR #35).** Zoom in and out (buttons, ⌘+/⌘−/⌘0, ctrl-scroll) and hold Space to pan. Draw with a pen, marker or highlighter in seven inks. Add pictures from the device by picking, dropping or pasting them. Paste or type a link and it becomes a card with its title and picture. 32 new stickers, food cut-outs among them (sushi, ramen, onigiri, dumpling, boba, ice cream, croissant, pretzel, beer, pizza). Page ideas: a type specimen, a Dada letterhead, a magazine spread, a photo wall, bits and pieces, a postcard, "What we did that day" (pick a day, get its moments in order with its photos), and a tier list of every place, every moment, or one moment's stops. Link addresses must be web addresses, checked on save.
- **Journal right-click menu (PR #35).** Right-click a thing for write, its style, tone, cut out a sticker, cut/copy/paste/duplicate, order (front, forward, backward, back), turn (straighten, tip, quarter turn), lock and delete; right-click bare paper to add something right there, paste there, change the paper, add a page, lock the page, save it as a picture or read. ⌘C, ⌘X and ⌘V work too.
- **Journal (PR #35).** The canvas comes back as a journal of A4 pages, made for you: a cover, then a page for each moment as it is added, laid out like a scrapbook (taped prints, typed notes, Dada and ransom titles, postmarks, tickets, a map of the moment's stops that plays). Make mode moves, sizes and turns anything with snapping, retypes titles in seven styles, adds 15 kinds of ephemera, locks a page or the whole journal, cuts stickers out of photos on the device (MediaPipe), and exports an A4 PDF, a page as a picture, or a journal file to add back. Read mode turns it like a book. The experience's sections (Moments, Journal, Shared, Activity) moved from tabs into the sidebar.
- **Simplification, part A (PR #34).** The product is cut back to one way in and one way out: Add photos or a sentence in the box; a story scroll with the film behind Play. Gone: the canvas editor (its paper visuals stay for the story), MCP assistants, quota requests, the itinerary table with its filters, three views and bulk select, the review sheet, the "Add moment" form path and per-row menus. New: a plain timeline by day with search; the form edits and deletes one moment. Backend: five tables stop being written (removed in part A2), `MOMENT_LIMIT = 300` replaces per-experience limits, `http.ts` is empty (auth routes come from `convex.config.ts`).
